# Freelance Management Portal

A web application designed to help freelancers manage their client relationships and business operations. The portal provides a centralized platform for tracking client information, managing contacts, and organizing business data.

## Features

### Authentication

- Sign in with Google or GitHub accounts
- Optional email and password authentication
- Secure session management

### Client Management

- **Add Clients**: Create new client records with contact information, company details, and notes
- **View Clients**: Browse your client list in an organized table view
- **Filter Clients**: View active clients, archived clients, or all clients
- **Edit Clients**: Update client information at any time
- **Archive Clients**: Hide clients from your active view without deleting them
- **Delete Clients**: Permanently remove clients from your database

### Client Information

Each client record can store:

- Name and email (required)
- Company name
- Phone number
- Website URL
- Notes and additional information

### Data Security

- All client data is private to your account
- Secure authentication protects your information
- Only you can access and manage your clients
